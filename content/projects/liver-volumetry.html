Task-Aware Multi-Model Probability Fusion with Uncertainty-Guided Review for Reliable Liver Volumetry

First author, under review
  • Medical imaging
  • Segmentation
  • Uncertainty

The model with the best Dice score is not the one with the best volume estimate, so a single overlap metric cannot stand in for surgical risk.

Approach

The probabilities of four U-Nets, in 2D, 2.5D and 3D, are fused in a task-aware way, and an uncertainty-guided review then targets the riskiest voxels.

Results

The fusion outperforms a per-case oracle that picks the best single network: Dice rises from 0.9238 to 0.9438 and volume error falls from 101.6 to 69.7 mL. Reviewing the riskiest 10% of voxels catches 70% of what remains, cutting false negatives from 4.18% to 1.49%.

MOSFET Characterization and CMOS Inverter Design

Graduate course project, ECE1388
  • CMOS
  • Layout
  • DRC / LVS

One small circuit, taken through the full custom flow: characterize the transistors, extract a delay model, size the inverter, lay it out, verify it, and measure what the layout changed.

Device characterization

From simulated nMOS and pMOS I–V curves I extracted a threshold voltage of Vt = 0.27 V (Vbs = 0 V, Vds = 0.1 V) and an off-current of 1.213 nA (Vgs = 0 V, Vds = 1 V). The curves also show velocity saturation in the shorter device, exponential subthreshold conduction, DIBL raising the subthreshold current at higher Vds, and the body effect shifting Vt.

Delay model

To extract the effective gate capacitance, I swept a load capacitance until the delay into it matched the delay into a real gate. That gave 9.63 fF for rising and 8.84 fF for falling edges, an average of Cg = 9.23 fF. From fanout-of-3 and fanout-of-4 delays (FO4: 15.75 ps rising, 15.29 ps falling) I fitted effective resistances RN = 212 Ω and RP = 232 Ω. These are effective values fitted to simulated delays, not fixed device constants.

Sizing

With Wn = 32, I swept the total pMOS width from 7.68 to 11.52 µm and measured both noise margins at the points where the transfer curve’s slope is −1. They cross at 9.503 µm (Wp ≈ 79.2), which I rounded to Wp = 80: Wp / Wn = 2.5, NMH = 333.9 mV and NML = 335.3 mV. For comparison, with Wp = 64 they were 349.2 and 322.0 mV. The pMOS is wider because holes have lower mobility than electrons.

Layout and verification

I drew a four-finger layout that is clean in Calibre DRC and LVS, then simulated it after extraction.

Results

Compared with the one-finger schematic, NMH rose by 12.8 mV (3.8%) to 346.7 mV, and NML by 3.4 mV (1.0%) to 338.7 mV. These differences may come from the four-finger layout’s geometry-dependent transistor parameters and parasitic resistance near switching; parasitic capacitance does not affect the steady-state transfer curve.

All results are from simulation and physical verification; no silicon was measured.

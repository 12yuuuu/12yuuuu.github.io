Beyond Accuracy and Calibration: Confidence Ranking Preservation and Routing Consistency for Fixed-budget Deferral in Quantized Edge AI

First author, under review
  • Quantization
  • Edge AI
  • Routing

Quantized models are checked for accuracy and calibration, but a fixed-budget router uses neither. It acts on the ordering of the confidence scores.

The question

Many edge systems run a small model first and defer the inputs it is least confident about to a larger model, up to a fixed budget. After quantization, does the small model still defer the same inputs? Accuracy and calibration do not answer that question.

What I did

The study covers per-channel, round-to-nearest, weight-only post-training quantization of ResNet-50, MobileNetV3-Large and ViT-B/16, with an activation-quantization check. My focus was the quantization study and the evaluation of ranking and routing behavior.

What we found

Across ResNet-50, MobileNetV3-L and ViT-B/16, accuracy degrades one to two bit-widths before the ordering does.

Preserving the ranking is not enough on its own, either. For ResNet-50 at 7 bits the ranking is statistically equivalent to full precision, yet the deferred set overlaps the full-precision one with a Jaccard index of only 0.371: about 46% of the deferred inputs are replaced.

The payoff is only safe to claim once all four properties are tested: accuracy, calibration, ranking and routing consistency. In a separate cascade experiment, where a MobileNetV3-L router defers to a full-precision ResNet-50, the 8-bit router cuts nominal weight storage from 20.9 MB to 5.3 MB, about 75% less. At the same 42.7% deferral budget, cascade accuracy is 80.39% with the FP32 router and 80.40% with the 8-bit one.

Caveats

The storage figure is nominal weight storage, not measured energy, latency or total memory; real-device energy and latency are the next measurements. The 42.7% budget was chosen on the evaluation split, so the cascade result is exploratory. The ResNet-50 overlap result and the MobileNetV3-L cascade come from separate experiments.

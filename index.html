01

Efficient & Trustworthy ML

Compression and automation are accepted on metrics that deployed systems do not actually consume. I measure the gap.

Beyond Accuracy and Calibration: Confidence Ranking Preservation and Routing Consistency for Fixed-Budget Deferral in Quantized Edge AI

First author · Under review

A quantized model is accepted on accuracy and calibration, but a fixed-budget router reads neither. It reads the ordering the confidence score induces, and across ResNet-50, MobileNetV3-L and ViT-B/16 accuracy degrades one to two bit-widths before that ordering does. Ranking alone is not sufficient either: at 7-bit ResNet-50 the ranking is statistically equivalent to full precision while only 0.371 of the deferred set is reproduced. Testing all four properties is what makes the payoff safe to claim: an 8-bit router cuts memory four times over, 20.9 MB to 5.3 MB, and raises compute saving from 51.7% to 57.7% at matched accuracy.

Beyond Global Rank Statistics: Localized Certification for Quantile-Thresholded Decision Systems

Second author · Under review

Anything that thresholds a score at a quantile, from CFAR detection to selective classification, is certified with global rank statistics. Those integrate over the whole score distribution while the operator acts at a single point, so the certificate can look near-perfect while the decision is rewritten: rank correlation above 0.98 alongside more than 60% of the selected set replaced. A displacement scale measured locally at the operating quantile predicts that churn across 3,570 operating points and thirteen perturbation families at R-squared 0.968, where the global control reaches 0.655.

Task-Aware Multi-Model Probability Fusion with Uncertainty-Guided Review for Reliable Liver Volumetry

First author · Under review

The Dice-optimal model and the volume-optimal model are not the same model, so a single overlap metric cannot stand in for surgical risk. Task-aware probability fusion over four 2D, 2.5D and 3D U-Nets beats a per-case oracle that picks the best single network (Dice 0.9238 to 0.9438, volume error 101.6 to 69.7 mL). Reviewing the riskiest 10% of voxels catches 70% of what remains, cutting false negatives from 4.18% to 1.49%.

02

Computer Vision

Recovering geometry and motion from ordinary cameras, without the calibration or the markers such systems usually assume.

Uncalibrated Multi-View Human Pose Estimation

First author · Manuscript in preparation

Multi-view 3D pose normally assumes the cameras have already been calibrated. Recovering camera pose at inference time instead, with no training, reaches AP25 95.83 on CMU Panoptic, above calibrated and fully supervised VoxelPose, MvP, 3DSA and MVGFormer. It also holds 85.4 AP25 when the camera arrangement is changed, where MVGFormer falls to 74.7.

A Motor-Cognitive Performance Assessment System Based on Markerless Pose Estimation and Real-Time Computer Vision

Co-author · Measurement (Elsevier), under review

Reaction and movement time in athletic testing are still measured by stopwatch or by strapping sensors to the athlete. A single-camera markerless pipeline measures them from video instead, running at 42 ms latency and resolving event times to within 59 ms.

03

Hardware & FPGA

Designing the accelerator and the model as one system, rather than compressing a model after the fact and hoping it maps well.

FPGA Acceleration of Mamba Inference

Graduate research, in progress

Selective scan is bound by memory and data movement rather than arithmetic, which makes a state space model a very different accelerator target from a transformer. I am working out where those bottlenecks actually sit in Mamba inference, aiming at an algorithmic or architectural contribution validated on hardware rather than a port of a published design.

Hardware-Accelerated LBP Facial Recognition System

Verilog RTL · VLSI design project

An RTL accelerator for Local Binary Pattern feature extraction and matching, built around an SRAM access pattern chosen to keep the datapath fed. Verified through gate-level simulation under a 0.34 ns clock constraint at 82.4% lint coverage.

About

I’m a master’s student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto, taking reconfigurable computing and FPGA architecture alongside VLSI design methodology. Before that I read Electrical Engineering at National Cheng Kung University in Taiwan.

My work so far has run along two tracks. One asks when a compressed or automated vision system can actually be trusted: which property a deployed system consumes, and whether the metric we accept it on measures that property at all. The other is the hardware underneath: RTL design, FPGA deployment, and two internships spent inside semiconductor manufacturing.

Those two tracks have met. My current work is FPGA acceleration of Mamba inference, where the cost is memory and data movement rather than arithmetic, and where the question I find interesting is what the model should look like if the hardware is being designed at the same time.
